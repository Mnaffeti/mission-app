# SkyLine Airlines Policy Manual

## customer_compensation

### Flight Delay Compensation Policy §1
keywords: delay, delayed, late, wait, waiting, hours, compensation, voucher
Passengers experiencing delays of 2–3 hours are entitled to meal vouchers and a €250 compensation payment for short-haul flights (<1500 km), €400 for medium-haul (1500–3500 km), and €600 for long-haul (>3500 km) under EU 261/2004. Staff must proactively inform affected passengers at the gate.

### Flight Cancellation & Rebooking Policy §2
keywords: cancel, cancelled, cancellation, refund, rebook, reroute, hotel, overnight
In the event of a cancellation, passengers must be offered: (a) a full refund within 7 business days, (b) re-routing at the earliest opportunity, or (c) re-routing at a later date of the passenger's choice. Hotel accommodation and transport to/from the hotel must be arranged for cancellations requiring an overnight stay.

### Miles & Loyalty Compensation Policy §3
keywords: miles, points, loyalty, frequent, flyer, goodwill, apology, sorry
As a goodwill gesture beyond statutory rights, customer service agents may award 2000–10000 bonus miles for significant service failures. Awards above 5000 miles require supervisor approval. Miles must be credited within 48 hours of the decision.

## baggage_handling

### Lost Baggage Procedure §1
keywords: lost, missing, baggage, bag, luggage, suitcase, pir, claim
A Property Irregularity Report (PIR) must be filed at the airport before the passenger leaves the baggage claim area. The airline has 21 days to locate lost baggage. If not recovered within 21 days, the bag is declared permanently lost and the passenger is entitled to compensation up to the Montreal Convention limit of SDR 1,288 (~$1,700 USD).

### Delayed Baggage Policy §2
keywords: delayed bag, bag delay, late bag, luggage delayed, essential, toiletries, clothing, receipt
Passengers whose baggage is delayed beyond 24 hours may claim essential items expenses (clothing, toiletries) up to €100 per day for a maximum of 3 days, with receipts required. The passenger must submit the claim form within 21 days of the baggage being returned.

### Damaged Baggage Policy §3
keywords: damaged, broken, damage, scratched, torn, handle, wheel, zipper
Damaged baggage must be reported within 7 days of receipt. Passengers must provide photos of the damage and the original purchase receipt or estimated value. Compensation covers repair costs; if repair is not possible, replacement value up to SDR 1,288 is paid. Damage to fragile or over-packed items is excluded from liability.

## safety_incidents

### Safety Incident Reporting Policy §1
keywords: safety, unsafe, danger, dangerous, risk, hazard, incident, accident, emergency
All safety-related incidents must be reported to the designated Safety Officer within 1 hour of occurrence. Staff must not dismiss or downplay passenger safety complaints. Reports are logged in the Safety Management System (SMS) and reviewed within 24 hours. Serious incidents are escalated to the Civil Aviation Authority (CAA) within 72 hours per regulatory requirements.

### Medical Emergency On-Board Policy §2
keywords: medical, sick, illness, hurt, injured, health, doctor, ambulance, divert
In the event of a medical emergency, the crew must: (1) assess and provide first aid, (2) use the on-board medical kit if trained to do so, (3) request assistance from any medical professionals on board, (4) contact ground medical support via radio, and (5) divert the flight if required. A full incident report must be filed post-flight.

## staff_conduct

### Complaint Against Crew Member Policy §1
keywords: rude, staff, crew, steward, stewardess, attendant, impolite, unprofessional, behavior, behaviour, attitude
Complaints regarding crew member behavior must be logged within 48 hours. The relevant department manager must acknowledge the complaint within 24 hours and initiate a formal investigation within 5 business days. Passengers must receive a written response within 15 business days detailing the outcome and any corrective action taken.

### Discrimination & Harassment Policy §2
keywords: discriminat, harass, racist, sexist, disability, religion, nationality, treated differently, unfair, prejudice
Any complaint involving discrimination or harassment is treated as a priority complaint. It must be escalated to HR and the Legal department within 24 hours. A formal written apology from management must be issued regardless of investigation outcome. Retraining or disciplinary action will be considered based on findings.

### Service Quality Standards §3
keywords: service, poor service, ignored, no help, unhelpful, disrespectful, complaint, experience
All passenger-facing staff are required to maintain courteous, professional communication at all times. If a passenger reports a negative experience with staff, the supervisor on duty must follow up with the passenger within the same day if still on premises. A formal apology and service recovery action (upgrade, voucher, miles) should be offered.
